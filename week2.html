Week 2:

This week, I developed a comprehensive foundation in programming and algorithmic thinking across multiple platforms and methodologies. I began by studying algorithms and flowchart design to strengthen my problem decomposition and logical reasoning skills, understanding how to break down complex problems into manageable, sequential steps. I then progressed to practical programming with Python, gaining hands-on experience with core programming concepts, syntax, data structures and control flow mechanisms that form the foundation of software development. To broaden my technical perspective, I explored visual programming through the Scratch platform, completing a project that deepened my understanding of event-driven logic, block-based development and how abstract programming concepts translate into interactive applications. Building on this foundation, I advanced to designing and developing a functional mobile application using MIT App Inventor, where I synthesized my algorithmic knowledge with user interface design principles and real-world application architecture. The hands-on experience of translating an idea from concept to working prototype reinforced my understanding of the development lifecycle. The week concluded with comprehensive training on the Forge work tool, which introduced me to a structured, systematic methodology for analyzing, dissecting and approaching complex engineering problem statements. This tool proved invaluable in teaching me how to extract key requirements, identify constraints and develop strategic solutions. Collectively, this progression reinforced my ability to think algorithmically, translate theoretical concepts into practical implementations and approach problems with structured methodology competencies essential for success in engineering design, software development and technology driven problemsolving.
