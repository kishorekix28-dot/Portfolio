Overview:

5S Activity (Robotics Team)

As part of the Week 1 ProtoSem activities, I actively participated in the implementation of the 5S workplace organization methodology within the Robotics team. Our responsibility was to organize and classify various robotics components, including motors, FluxGen modules, sensors, controllers, and other electronic hardware.

The components were carefully sorted, identified, and arranged in their designated storage racks to improve accessibility, inventory management, and workplace efficiency. This activity reinforced the importance of maintaining an organized workspace, reducing search time, and ensuring that components are readily available for ongoing projects.

Through this hands-on experience, I also gained practical exposure to several robotics components and developed a better understanding of the hardware commonly used in projects at Forge. The activity highlighted the significance of systematic organization and teamwork in creating an efficient engineering workspace.
