The Zeroth Week marked the beginning of my ProtoSem journey, serving as an orientation phase to familiarize us with the program's objectives, learning methodology, and expectations. During this session, we gained a comprehensive understanding of ProtoSem and its unique approach to fostering innovation, problem-solving, and entrepreneurial thinking.

This session provided valuable insight into how innovation is transformed into impactful ventures.

Another reflective activity involved reading and connecting with a Zen Pencils article. We analyzed the story and related its message to our own life experiences, allowing us to reflect on our personal journey, values, challenges, and aspirations. This exercise emphasized the importance of self-reflection, perseverance, and continuous personal growth.

Overall, the Zeroth Week established a strong foundation for the ProtoSem program by combining orientation, self-discovery, and reflection, preparing us for the innovation-focused learning experiences in the weeks ahead.
